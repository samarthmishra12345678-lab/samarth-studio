Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 October 2026. This site is operated by Samarth Mishra, Nagpur, India.

What this site collects

When you sign in with Google, Google sends this site your name, email address, Google account id, and profile photo if Google provides one. When you send a brief, this site stores your name, email, phone number if you type one, the website type you chose, budget range, timeline, and your notes.

The server also stores the time of each login, a session cookie, and the browser user-agent string so the operator can see who signed in.

How it is used

Records are used only to reply to your brief, quote a price, and keep a log of work. They are not sold, not used for ads, and not sent to marketing tools.

Storage

Data sits in a SQLite file on the server that hosts this site. Google sign-in is verified with Google. Session cookies are httpOnly and last up to 14 days.

What this site does not do

No advertising pixels, no analytics counters, no sale of lists, no public display of other clients’ names.

Your choices

Email the operator using the address on your order confirmation thread (the same address you used to sign in) and ask to delete your account, logins, and briefs. Deletion is done by hand from the studio inbox.

Contact

Samarth Mishra, Nagpur. Use the booking form after Google login for project mail.
